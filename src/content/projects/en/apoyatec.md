## What is Margaunt?

Margaunt is a digital platform created to help businesses grow through modern technology solutions. Its purpose is to optimize internal processes, strengthen online presence, and improve customer interaction.

## 🎯 Project Goal

To provide a professional solution for companies looking to digitize operations, increase productivity, and deliver a modern user experience.

## 🚀 Main Features

- Modern responsive design.
- Intuitive admin dashboard.
- Customer and service management.
- Internal process automation.
- Real-time database integration.
